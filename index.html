Our Methodology

Three intelligence layers. One decisive output.

01
Live SERP Intelligence
We pull real-time Google results for your keyword via SerpAPI — extracting the top 10 organic positions with titles, URLs, and snippet data. No cached estimates.
02
Competitor Profiling
Each competing domain is scored for authority, backlink volume, and estimated traffic via DataForSEO — giving you a clear benchmark to target and surpass.
03
AI-Driven Strategy
GPT-4o synthesises all signals into a prioritised action plan: content gaps, technical fixes, link-building targets, and a realistic timeline to reach your target ranking.
